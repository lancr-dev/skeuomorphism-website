01 / The idea

Designed to feel familiar.

Skeuomorphism gives digital interfaces cues from objects we already know: a page to turn, a switch to flip, a dial to adjust.

Page 01

The material

Paper invites reading. Metal suggests precision. Wood gives a workspace warmth. Each surface carries a useful association.

A surface should have a purpose.

Page 02

The behavior

Raised buttons invite a press. Recessed tracks guide movement. Familiar visual cues make an action easier to recognize.
